Description

KELLY ESTATES are delighted to bring to the market this beautiful family home in *** IMMACULATE CONDITION THROUGHOUT *** A bright, modern and exceptional well presented 3 bedroom semi detached house adjacent to a large green.

No. 124 is a 3 bedroom, 3 bathroom semi detached home located in the popular Aylmer Park development. The property is located at the start of a quiet cul de sac and conveniently located a short stroll from Nass town. The accommodation is bright and very well laid out, briefly comprises of an entrance hall, living room, guest w/c, family bathroom, kitchen and dining area, on the first floor, landing leading to three bedrooms (master-ensuite) and is presented in very good condition throughout. The property is approached by a off street parking to the front and benefits from a mature rear garden enclosed with block built walls.

Aylmer Park is a residential development of houses situated just off the Monread Road. An ideal opportunity for those expanding families looking for a beautiful home easily accessible to all amenities. Situated in a very popular and well located development, The house is only a short walk from Monread Shopping Centre with Tesco, Argos, Boots, Aldi and McDonalds.

Naas offers a wide selection of amenities with restaurants, pubs, local retailers that include ODEON Cinema, Lidl, Powercity, B & Q, Harvey Norman, Woodies, Halfords all within a short drive. Excellent transport links with easy access to the N7/M7 junction 9 and Sallins arrow/train services
